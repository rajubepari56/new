01 / The problem

Broken tracking means you pay for ads and can't tell which ones work.

  1. Your reports do not match. Google Ads, GA4 and your shop or CRM each show different leads or sales.
  2. Smart Bidding learns from bad data. Missing or duplicate conversions teach it to chase the wrong clicks.
  3. Meta and iOS lose your sales. Purchases arrive late, twice, or not at all, so you cut the campaigns that were working.
