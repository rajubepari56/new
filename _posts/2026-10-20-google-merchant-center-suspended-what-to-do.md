**Key takeaways**

- Read the exact reason in Merchant Center before changing anything.
- Most suspensions come from missing store information, data that does not match your site or policy issues.
- Fix the cause first, then request a review. Google makes the final decision and the timing is not in your control.

## Start with the message

Open the Diagnostics or Needs attention area. Google names the problem and often the affected products or pages. Write it down. Guessing and changing many things at once makes it harder to find what worked.

## Common causes

- **Missing or unclear store information:** shipping, returns, contact details or business identity.
- **Feed and website do not match:** price, availability or product details differ.
- **Policy issues:** content or claims that break advertising or product policies.
- **Website problems:** broken checkout, missing policy pages or an unreachable site.
- **Missing product data:** required attributes such as identifiers, images or condition.

## What to do, in order

1. Read the reason for every issue.
2. Fix the source: your website, your feed or your account settings.
3. Check a few products by hand, comparing the page with the feed.
4. Wait for the feed to refresh so the fixes are picked up.
5. Request a review once everything is fixed.

## While you wait

Keep your site live and consistent. Do not create a new account to get around a suspension, because that can make things worse. If you are asked for more information, answer clearly and exactly.
