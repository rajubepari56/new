**Key takeaways**

- A complete, accurate and active Business Profile helps customers choose you and helps Google understand you.

Your Google Business Profile is often the first thing a customer sees. These 12 fixes are a good starting order for a local service business.

## 1. Pick the right primary category

Your primary category tells Google what you are. Choose the one that best matches your main service, not the broadest one. Look at the categories used by top local competitors for ideas.

## 2. Add relevant secondary categories

Add only categories that describe services you actually offer. More is not better if they are not true.

## 3. List every service you offer

Add each real service, and write a short description for each. Use plain words customers use.

## 4. Write a clear business description

Explain who you are, what you do and where you work, in a natural voice, under the 750-character limit. Do not stuff keywords.

## 5. Check your business name

Use your real, legal or commonly used business name. Adding keywords or locations to the name can get a profile suspended.

## 6. Set accurate hours and special hours

Add holiday hours and any changes. Wrong hours lead to missed calls and bad reviews.

## 7. Set your service area correctly

If you travel to customers, add the areas you really serve. Follow Google's rules about showing or hiding your address.

## 8. Add attributes that are true

Add only attributes your business truly qualifies for.

## 9. Upload real photos regularly

Show your team, vehicles, tools and finished work. Name your files clearly, for example `kitchen-remodel-before.jpg`. Real photos build more trust than stock images.

## 10. Ask for reviews and reply to them

Ask happy customers after each job with a short message and a direct link. Reply to every review in a calm, helpful voice. Never buy, fake or trade reviews.

## 11. Keep your website link and details consistent

Link to the most relevant page, and use tracking parameters so you can see profile visits in analytics. Make sure your name, address and phone number match your website and the main directories.

## 12. Post updates

Share offers, news and recent jobs. Posts are optional, but they keep your profile fresh. Event and offer posts end on their dates.

## Common mistakes

- Choosing a category you want rather than the one that fits
- Keyword stuffing the name or description
- Changing many things at once, then not knowing what caused a problem
- Ignoring negative reviews

## How to measure it

Check Business Profile **calls, direction requests and website clicks** each month, and compare them with Search Console and GA4.
