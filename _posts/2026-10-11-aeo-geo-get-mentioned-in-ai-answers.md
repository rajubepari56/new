## The three terms

- **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization):** shaping your content so it gives short, direct answers that search features and assistants can quote.
- **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization):** making your content and brand useful to AI tools that write answers.
- **LLM optimization:** the same idea for large language models such as ChatGPT. In practice, I treat them as one project.

## What you can control

AI tools draw on what they can find about you: your website, your Google Business Profile, reviews, directories and mentions elsewhere. You can improve each of these.

## A simple plan

**1. Ask the questions your customers ask.** List 10 to 20 real questions, for example "Who is the best plumber in Brisbane?" or "How much does a roof repair cost?". Ask them in a few AI tools and note the answers. This is your starting point.

**2. Answer clearly on your site.** Put the direct answer in the first lines of the page, then give detail. Use question-style headings and short paragraphs.

**3. Add FAQs people really ask.** Real FAQs on service pages help customers and give machines clear question and answer pairs. Add FAQ schema where it fits.

**4. Add structured data.** Use schema such as Organization or LocalBusiness, Service and FAQPage so machines can read the facts on your page.

**5. Keep your facts consistent.** Your name, address, phone, services and areas should match across your website, Business Profile and directories. If they differ, AI tools may be unsure which is right.

**6. Build real proof.** Reviews, case studies, mentions and links from trusted sites help both search engines and AI tools trust you.

**7. Check crawler access.** Make sure your `robots.txt` does not block the bots you want to allow, and that important pages load without needing a login.

**8. Consider an `llms.txt` file (optional).** It is a plain summary of your site for AI tools. Support varies, so treat it as a small extra, not a ranking trick.

## What to avoid

- Promising a mention. Nobody can guarantee one.
- Copying or generating lots of thin pages
- Fake reviews or fake author profiles
- Stuffing pages with keywords

## How to measure

Each month, ask the same questions again and record whether you are named or linked. Check analytics for visits from AI tools. Compare with the starting point.

## Do traditional SEO and local SEO still matter?

Yes. Strong pages, a good Business Profile, speed and trusted links are still the base. AI search work builds on them.
