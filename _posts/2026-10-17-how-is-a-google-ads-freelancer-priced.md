## Your ad spend is not the fee

Your budget is paid by you directly to Google. The specialist's fee is for the work of planning, building, optimizing and reporting. Never hand over a card that the specialist controls, and keep the account in your name.

## The common pricing models

**Monthly retainer.** A fixed monthly fee for ongoing management. It is easy to budget. Ask what is included: calls, reports, new ads, tracking fixes.

**Percentage of ad spend.** The fee rises with your budget. It can be fair for large accounts, but make sure the work grows with the fee, and ask about a minimum.

**Hourly.** Useful for audits and small fixes. Ask for an estimate range, not an open meter.

**Fixed-price project.** Good for a defined job, such as a tracking setup or an account audit. The scope should be written clearly.

## What changes the price

- The size and complexity of the account
- The number of campaigns and products
- How much tracking needs to be fixed
- How often you want reports and calls
- Extra services, such as GA4, Tag Manager or server-side tracking

## What to ask before you agree

1. What exactly is included each month?
2. Are tracking setup and fixes included or extra?
3. How long is the commitment, and how do I end it?
4. Do I keep full access to every account?
5. What will I receive as proof of work?
