baseline: The Smart Campaign had spent about $1,200 at about $39 a day with a 2.39% CTR. Average CPC was A$0.37 and cost per store visit was A$9.13 on the Smart campaign (14-day comparison).
challenge: The owner wanted more bookings, calls, directions and store visits, backed by real sales data, and the Smart Campaign was not delivering them.
work:
- Audited the account first, covering structure, targeting, locations, search terms, bidding, budget, ad copy, schedule and competitors, and wrote a campaign plan the owner approved before launch.
- Built a new local Search campaign with UTM tracking, location targeting around the café, and an ad schedule that follows opening hours.
- Added dedicated ad groups for lunch and breakfast "near me" searches, plus a bid adjustment for the 8 to 10am breakfast window.
- Tested Performance Max for store visits, then split it into focused campaigns by theme.
- Kept the original Smart Campaign running at the owner's budget while testing, to protect day-to-day trade.
- Reviewed conversion tracking, found a Google auto-applied bidding change and reversed it, and flagged the gaps (form submits, click-to-call, enhanced conversions).
- Linked Google Analytics and the Business Profile to Google Ads, and cleaned up search terms and AI Max traffic with negative keywords.
- Compared weekly sales data from the café with ad results, and built a free Looker Studio dashboard for the owner.
