- Rebuilt the account around the product catalog and search intent after a full audit.

- Split Shopping and Performance Max campaigns by product family and margin tier, using Performance Max only where it added conversions.
- Launched non-brand Search campaigns for best-selling products, with strict negative lists and ROAS guardrails on competitor terms.
- Switched to value-based bidding (tROAS) fed with first-party audience signals and high-intent segments.
- Tightened geo-targeting and ad scheduling to top-converting provinces and the hours contractors are most active.
