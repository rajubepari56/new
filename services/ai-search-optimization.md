faq_title: AI search optimization questions
faqs:
- q: What do AEO, GEO and LLM optimization mean?
  a: 'AEO is Answer Engine Optimization: giving short, direct answers that search features can quote. GEO is Generative Engine Optimization: making your content useful to AI tools that write answers. LLM optimization is the same idea for large language models such as ChatGPT. I treat them as one project.'
- q: Is this a replacement for SEO?
  a: No. Good SEO, fast pages and trusted listings still matter. AI search work builds on them and adds clear answers, structured data and consistent brand facts.
- q: Can you guarantee ChatGPT or Google will mention me?
  a: No. AI tools change often and no one controls their answers. I improve how clearly and consistently your business can be understood, then track what the tools say.
- q: How do you measure results?
  a: I record answers to a fixed set of customer questions each month, note whether you are named or linked, and compare with the starting point. I also watch traffic that comes from AI tools in analytics.
- q: Do I need an llms.txt file?
  a: It is optional. Some sites add one as a plain summary for AI tools, but support varies. I can add it as a low-cost extra, and I focus first on pages, schema and consistent business data.
