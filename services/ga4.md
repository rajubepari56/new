faq_title: GA4 questions
faqs:
- q: Can you fix my existing GA4 property?
  a: Usually, yes. I audit first, then fix what is wrong instead of starting over, unless the setup is too broken to save.
- q: Why does GA4 show different conversions from Google Ads?
  a: They use different attribution and counting rules, and tracking errors often add to the gap. I check both sides and explain the difference in plain English.
- q: Do I need BigQuery?
  a: Not for most businesses. I only suggest it when you need raw data exports or advanced analysis.
- q: Will you set GA4 up through Google Tag Manager?
  a: Yes, wherever possible. It keeps changes documented and means you do not depend on a developer for every tweak.
- q: How long does a GA4 setup take?
