02 / Working together

What I need from you

  • A clear goal: more leads, higher ROAS or accurate data
  • Access to the accounts involved, in your name
  • Your real lead or order numbers to compare against
  • One person who can approve changes quickly

What you get from me

  • A written quote before work starts
  • Clear dates, and I keep them
  • Tested work and a documented handover
  • Quick replies and honest advice, including when you do not need me
